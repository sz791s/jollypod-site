Everything in the box

Useful features.
No feature soup.

JollyPod is designed around one idea: listening should be simple, but your setup can be as specific as you want.

01

Inbox + queue

New episodes land in an inbox instead of silently taking over. Choose what moves to your queue, put it in the right order, and know exactly what plays next.

02

Audio + video

Listen to regular audio podcasts and watch video podcasts in the same focused player. One library, one queue.

03

Chapter skipping

Skip chapters you do not want to hear. Configure the behaviour once and keep the useful parts of each episode.

04

Per-podcast settings

Different shows deserve different rules. Tune playback and episode behaviour for each podcast instead of settling for one global compromise.

05

Downloads + offline

Download episodes before you leave Wi-Fi and keep listening on trains, flights, hikes, or wherever the signal gives up.

06

Apple Watch

Control your listening from your wrist and keep the important bits within reach with dedicated Watch widgets.

07

Mac + Control Center

Use the Mac app when you are at your desk and reach the controls quickly through the system surfaces you already use.

08

MP3 sideloading

Not every good thing has an RSS feed. Bring your own MP3 files and listen to them alongside your podcasts.

09

OPML import + export

Move your subscriptions in and take them out again. Your podcast list is not a hostage.
