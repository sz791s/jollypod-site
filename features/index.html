Everything in the box

Useful features.
No feature soup.

JollyPod is designed around one idea: listening should be simple, but your setup can be as specific as you want.

01

Find what you want

Browse trending episodes and podcasts, switch countries, and add shows without fighting through a noisy discovery page.

02

Inbox first

New episodes land in an inbox instead of silently taking over. Swipe or open the menu to decide what deserves a place in the queue.

03

Queue in your order

Build the order yourself, drag episodes around, and know exactly what plays next. Your queue stays yours.

04

Skip chapters

Skip intros, ads, repeated segments, or anything else you do not want to hear. Configure it once and keep the useful parts.

05

Podcast-specific rules

Different shows deserve different behaviour. Queue next, queue last, pin to inbox, or use the default when a show does not need special treatment.

06

Audio + video player

Listen to audio podcasts and watch video podcasts in the same focused player, with chapters, sleep timer, playback speed, and AirPlay nearby.

07

On your phone, wrist, and Mac

Use JollyPod on iPhone, Apple Watch, and Mac. Keep the important controls close with Watch widgets and system surfaces like Control Center.

ALSO / OFFLINE

Downloads

Download episodes before you leave Wi-Fi and keep listening on trains, flights, hikes, or wherever the signal gives up.

ALSO / MP3

MP3 sideloading

Not every good thing has an RSS feed. Bring your own MP3 files and listen to them alongside your podcasts.

ALSO / OPML

OPML import + export

Move your subscriptions in and take them out again. Your podcast list is not a hostage.
