There is more

Optional full control.

JollyPod stays simple on the surface, with useful controls ready when you want them.

01 / DISCOVER

Find your favorite podcasts

Browse trending episodes and podcasts, switch countries, and add shows you like without following a podcast.

02 / INBOX

You decide what plays next

Add new episodes automatically to your inbox, queue next or queue last. Decide for all and podcast-specific. Swipe or open the menu to decide what deserves a place next in the queue.

03 / QUEUE

Your queue, your control

Build the order yourself, drag episodes around, and know exactly what plays next. Your queue stays yours.

04 / PLAYER

Audio and video podcasts

Listen to audio podcasts and watch video podcasts in the same focused player, with chapters, sleep timer, playback speed, and AirPlay support.

05 / CHAPTERS

Skip chapters

Skip intros, ads, repeated segments, or anything else you do not want to hear. Skip the boring parts and save time.

06 / SETTINGS

Settings for every podcast

Different shows deserve different behaviour. Queue next, queue last, pin to inbox, playback speed, and number of episodes to save: all settings can be default or podcast-specific.

07 / WIDGETS

Right on your Home Screen

Keep your queue close with Home Screen widgets and quick access to the episodes you actually want to hear next.

08 / EVERYWHERE

On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch

Use JollyPod across your devices. iPhone is the focus, with iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch app.

ALSO / OFFLINE

Downloads

Decide what and how much you want to download. Keep listening on trains, flights, hikes, or wherever the signal gives up.

ALSO / MP3

MP3 sideloading

Not every good thing has an RSS feed. Bring your own MP3 files and listen to them alongside your podcasts. Share directly from files or supported websites.

ALSO / OPML

OPML import + export

Move your subscriptions in and take them out again. Your podcast list is not a hostage.
