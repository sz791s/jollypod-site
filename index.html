The short version

Less feed.
More control.

Podcasts arrive in your inbox. You choose what enters the queue, how each show behaves, and what gets skipped. JollyPod handles the rest.

01 / QUEUE

An actual listening plan

Build the order yourself. Move episodes around. Keep the inbox separate from what plays next.

02 / RULES

Each podcast, configured

Set playback behaviour per show and skip chapters you never want to hear again.

03 / ANYWHERE

Phone, wrist, Mac

Keep listening from your Apple Watch, Watch widgets, Mac app, or Control Center.

For the organised listener

Built to be predictable.

Your queue stays yours. Downloads are explicit. Imports and exports use OPML. MP3 files are welcome too.

  • queue.sort(user_intent)
  • chapters.skip(your_rules)
  • downloads.keep(offline)
  • subscriptions.export(opml)
For normal humans too

Still just press play.

All that control stays out of the way when you do not need it. Pick an episode, tap play, continue with your day.
