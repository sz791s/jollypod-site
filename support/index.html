Quick answers

Frequently asked

How do I install the current beta?

Install Apple’s TestFlight app, then open the JollyPod invitation on your Apple device and follow the prompts.

How does the annual subscription work?

JollyPod is free to use and offers an optional paid annual subscription. The exact price and included benefits are shown in the app before purchase. Apple handles payment, renewal, cancellation, and refunds under its store rules.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open Settings on your Apple device, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Choose JollyPod to view or change the subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I listen offline?

Yes. Download episodes before leaving your connection. Availability of individual episodes still depends on the podcast publisher.

Can I move subscriptions from another app?

Yes. Export an OPML file from your existing podcast app and import it into JollyPod. You can also export your JollyPod subscriptions again later.

A podcast or episode is missing. What now?

Podcast listings and media come from their publishers. First check whether the show’s feed still works elsewhere. If it does, email the feed or episode link to support and it can be investigated.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include your device model, operating system version, JollyPod version, steps that reproduce the problem, and a screenshot if it helps. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.
